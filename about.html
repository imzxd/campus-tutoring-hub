Business Case

Campus Tutoring Hub is designed to provide students with a centralized platform for accessing tutoring and academic support services. Users can explore available services, submit detailed service requests (including attachments), rate completed sessions, and manage their activity history—all in one place.

This platform aims to simplify the process of connecting students with academic assistance while maintaining a user-friendly and efficient interface.
